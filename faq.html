Home / FAQ

Questions, answered.

A few things people ask before their first visit. Anything else, just call the shop.

Do you work on all makes?

We work on cars and light trucks of most makes, domestic and import. If you are not sure whether we service your model, give us a call and we will let you know.

Do I need an appointment?

Yes. Quality runs walk in or by appointment so your car gets dedicated time on the lift. Call the shop or use the request form and we will confirm a time by phone.

What are your hours?

Monday to Friday, 8:00am to 7:00pm. Closed Saturday and Sunday.

Where are you located?

1685 Babcock St, Ste B, Costa Mesa, CA 92627. See the map on our Visit page for directions.

How do I get an estimate?

Call and describe the symptom, or bring the car in. For anything beyond routine service, we diagnose first and give you a clear plan and estimate before any work begins.

Do you offer a warranty on repairs?

Yes. We stand behind our work. Ask us about the warranty on parts and labor for your specific repair when you drop the car off.

Still have a question?

Call the shop and we will give you a straight answer.
