A neighborhood shop you can trust.

Quality is a small Costa Mesa shop that looks after cars and trucks of most makes, walk in or by appointment.

Our approach

Careful work, every time.

Working on the same jobs every day builds a level of care and speed you can feel. We know the common jobs and the common failure points because we see them every day.

Because we run walk in or by appointment, your car gets scheduled time on the lift instead of a place in a waiting line. You know when to drop it off, and we know exactly what we are doing that day.

Most of all, we give straight answers. Drivers on Yelp say the same thing again and again: fair prices and honest advice. We would rather tell you a repair can wait than sell you one you do not need.
